Henry Roman

Ucr Biology Student📚

My name is Henry Roman, and I am a biology major at the University of California, Riverside. My academic journey is guided by a desire to understand how living organisms function, interact, and adapt to their environments. Biology interests me because it connects questions about life to discoveries that can improve our understanding of the world. As I continue my undergraduate education, I am building a foundation in scientific reasoning, laboratory methods, and communication. My long-term career goal is to become a biology professor who contributes to research while helping students develop confidence in their ability to learn science.

One of the aspects of biology that I find most meaningful is the connection between different levels of life. Processes within a single cell can influence an entire organism, while interactions among organisms can shape whole ecosystems. Studying these connections encourages me to approach problems with curiosity and consider multiple explanations before reaching a conclusion. I want to continue exploring different areas of biology as I discover the research questions that interest me most. For me, education is an opportunity to move beyond memorizing information and learn how to ask thoughtful questions, evaluate evidence, and explain why a result matters.

Through my coursework at UCR, I am developing skills that will support my future academic and professional goals. Science courses and laboratory assignments provide opportunities to practice interpreting results, following procedures, and communicating findings. Working through challenging material also teaches me patience and the importance of preparation. When an explanation or experiment does not produce an expected outcome, I see value in reviewing the process and identifying what could be improved. I aim to become a more independent learner while remaining open to feedback from instructors and classmates. These habits will be important as I prepare for more advanced study and research.

My goal of becoming a professor reflects my interest in both scientific discovery and education. I hope to create a classroom environment where students feel comfortable asking questions and working through difficult concepts. Biology can seem overwhelming when unfamiliar terminology and complex processes are introduced together. As a future educator, I would like to make these ideas more approachable through clear explanations, practical examples, and opportunities for discussion. I also hope to mentor students as they explore their interests and consider their own educational paths. I believe effective teaching requires listening, flexibility, and a willingness to keep learning alongside others.

Looking ahead, I plan to pursue graduate education and seek opportunities to strengthen my research experience. I want to improve my ability to design investigations, analyze data, and communicate scientific ideas to different audiences. Along the way, I hope to learn from faculty members, collaborate with other students, and develop a clearer sense of my future research focus. My experience at the University of California, Riverside is an important step toward these goals. Ultimately, I want to build a career that brings together curiosity, thoughtful research, and dedicated teaching while making a positive contribution to the students and academic communities I serve.
